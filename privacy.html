Privacy Notice

Pending privacy review. This preliminary notice is not an approved replacement for CEGY's complete privacy policy. Do not submit utility account numbers or sensitive personal information through the website or external calculator.

PHEEP.us offers email links to contact CEGY but no embedded enrollment form. Emails are handled by the sender's and recipient's email systems.

The separate calculator may ask for site addresses and operating information to produce estimates; it uses third-party address lookup and historical-weather services. It has separate access controls for staff reports.

External sites and image providers may process standard web-request data. CEGY must finalize its data retention, disclosure, contact, and rights practices and obtain applicable SoCalGas Privacy and Policy review before collecting private program customer information.

Contact steve@cegy.us with privacy questions.
