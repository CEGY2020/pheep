Pool Heating Energy Efficiency Program

Smarter Pool Heating. Better Visibility. Lower Energy Waste.

PHEEP provides qualifying commercial and institutional swimming pools with intelligent monitoring and control designed to improve pool-heating efficiency, provide operational visibility, and support better energy management.

Request a Site Review See How It Works

What PHEEP Provides

  • Pool heating monitoring and control
  • Temperature and runtime data
  • Operational reports and alerts
  • Predictive scheduling to reduce unnecessary heating
  • Direct-install support for qualifying sites

What Is PHEEP?

PHEEP is focused on improving the efficiency and visibility of swimming-pool heating systems at eligible facilities.

Monitor

Track pool temperature, outdoor conditions, heater outlet temperature, runtime, and other operating information.

Control

Use intelligent scheduling and predictive start strategies to reduce avoidable heater operation while maintaining target pool conditions.

Report

Receive useful operating information, alerts, and reports that help facility teams understand how the pool-heating system is performing.

Potential Benefits

Actual results vary by pool, operating schedule, equipment, weather, utility rates, and existing controls.

Reduced Heating Waste

Better control can help avoid heating the pool earlier or longer than necessary.

Improved Operational Insight

Continuous data helps identify abnormal temperatures, excessive runtime, and other operating conditions that deserve attention.

Remote Visibility

Facility teams can gain a clearer picture of pool-heating operation without relying only on manual checks.

How It Works

The process is designed to be straightforward for the facility.

1
Site Review

We review the pool, heater, operating schedule, and basic facility information.

2
Qualification

We determine whether the site appears to meet current program requirements.

3
Installation

The Pool Control System is installed and connected to the pool-heating equipment.

4
Monitoring

Operating data, reports, and alerts are used to support ongoing energy-management decisions.

Who May Qualify?

PHEEP is intended for qualifying commercial and institutional pools within the applicable service territory and program requirements.

  • Hotels and hospitality properties
  • Multifamily communities
  • Country clubs and HOAs
  • Private schools and educational facilities
  • Swim schools and nonprofit facilities
  • Other qualifying commercial pools

What We Usually Need

To begin a site review, it helps to have:

  • Pool address
  • Approximate pool dimensions or square footage
  • Pool operating hours and target temperature
  • Heater make, model, and input rating
  • A recent gas bill, when readily available
